# Getting started

## Sign in and set up
1. Click **Continue with Google**. Scout only asks for your name, email and profile picture.
2. Pick your **country** (Kenya, United States or South Africa) and **currency**.
3. Choose the **retailers** to search. Only retailers that serve your country are listed, and all of them are selected by default.
4. Choose your **default AI model**. You can pick a different model for each wishlist later.
5. Optionally **link your shopping accounts** by recording the email you use at each retailer. Linking is informational for now: Scout doesn't log in to or buy from those sites.

You can change all of this later in **Settings**.

## Add items
1. Click **New wishlist**, give it a name, and keep or change the AI model.
2. Open the wishlist and click **Add item**. Only a title is required. A budget, brands to include or exclude, condition, size/colour, a reference link and a ship-to country all help the search.
3. Saving starts a search. The item shows **Scouting…**, per-retailer progress chips, and candidates appear as each one is checked.

## Review matches
- Each card shows the retailer, price (with an approximate conversion when it's in another currency), availability, when the price was checked, the match score, and **why?**, the agent's explanation.
- Prices and links always come from the live product page, never from the AI.
- **✓ Approve** marks your pick (you can approve more than one). **✗ Reject** with an optional reason: reasons are used in the next search. **☆ Save** keeps it for later.
- Use the retailer chips to filter or group results. "Independent store" marks a Shopify shop; "Ships internationally" warns about import duties.
- **Find more** runs a new search. Editing the budget or other search details offers a new search too.
- Set **Re-scout** to daily or weekly to get notified about new or cheaper matches.

## Your data
**Settings ▸ Your data** lets you download everything Scout stores about you, or delete your account and all its data.
